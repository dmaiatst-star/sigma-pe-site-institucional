Our Mission

SIGMA P&E was born from hands-on experience on the factory floor and evolved into strategic engineering. Our goal is to unite productive efficiency with maximum occupational safety and team capacity building.

With deep academic and practical know-how, we develop training programs, physical and cognitive demand studies, and risk management, ensuring your company operates in compliance with legal requirements without losing operational dynamics.

Frequently Asked Questions

Get answers to your main questions about our services and current legislation.

The PGR (Risk Management Program) replaced the PPRA with a much more comprehensive and continuous management approach. While the PPRA focused almost exclusively on environmental risks (physical, chemical, and biological), the PGR integrates and requires the mapping of ergonomic and accident risks as well, unifying Health and Safety management in an integrated and effective manner.

Yes! One of our greatest specialties is bringing training into the client's work environment. This optimizes your team's time, eliminates travel costs, and makes learning much more practical, using the actual machinery and operational routine as a training laboratory.

Using validated scientific methods (such as NASA-TLX), our Production Engineering team identifies cognitive fatigue bottlenecks (mental fatigue and stress under pressure). By understanding these metrics, we can balance production lines, prevent serious operational errors, and preserve operators' mental health, meeting NR-01 requirements and elevating factory efficiency.

We provide training on the main Regulatory Standards, with emphasis on NR-05 (CIPA), NR-10 (Electricity), NR-11 (Material Handling), NR-12 (Machinery and Equipment), NR-23 (Fire Protection), and NR-35 (Work at Heights). Additionally, we develop customized safety culture (DDS) training and internal campaigns.
