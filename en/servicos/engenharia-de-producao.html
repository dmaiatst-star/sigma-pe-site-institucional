Technical Differentiator

Our Production Engineering team uses cutting-edge scientific protocols, such as NASA-TLX (Task Load Index) from NASA, to assess cognitive and physical workloads in the workplace. This allows us to size teams with scientific precision, identify overloads before they cause health damage or productivity drops, and propose engineering solutions that unite operational efficiency with comprehensive worker protection. Each analysis is grounded in objective data, ensuring evidence-based management decisions.
